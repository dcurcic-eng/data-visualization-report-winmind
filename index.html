Executive summary
Meta is carrying the funnel; search has stalled since late July.

Since launch on Jun 24, Winmind has invested $2,667 across both channels and produced 11 verified leads at a blended cost of $242. Meta contributed 9 of the 11 on 55% of the spend.

Meta spent $1,469 to reach 6,925 people across 17.0K impressions (frequency 2.46), producing 9 leads at $163.27 each, at CPC $2.38 and CPM $86.43. Google spent $1,198 on 2.5K impressions and 110 clicks for 2 conversions at $598.87. Search buys a stronger CTR (4.37%) but at 4.6× Meta's click cost, and it has had zero delivery since Jul 29.

Next lever: search has been dark for 19 days. Restarting it, or moving that budget into Meta, is the largest single change available to lead volume this month.
